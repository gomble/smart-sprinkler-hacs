# Smart Sprinkler

Control your garden irrigation with zone scheduling, weather-based automation, and a beautiful Lovelace card.

**Features:**
- Multiple zones with individual schedules
- Weather-based skip (rain, wind, frost)
- Pump & master valve support
- Soak & cycle mode
- Rain delay
- Custom Lovelace card

See the [README](https://github.com/gomble/smart-sprinkler-hacs) for full documentation.
